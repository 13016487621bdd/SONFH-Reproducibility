Figure 2. Individual functional gene-set associations with established SONFH. (A) Sex-adjusted disease coefficients (points) and HC3-based 95% confidence intervals (horizontal lines) for the eight gene sets meeting Benjamini–Hochberg FDR < 0.05 among all 85 prespecified tests. Coefficients use original GSVA score units and represent SONFH relative to glucocorticoid-exposed participants without SONFH; the dashed vertical line denotes zero. Short process labels correspond to the full identifiers in Supplementary Tables S1 and S2. (B) Scores for the same gene sets in the same row order. Each column represents one participant. Controls precede SONFH cases; within each group, the archived sample order is retained. No clustering was applied. For display, each row was standardized using its mean and sample standard deviation across all 40 participants. Blue indicates below-cohort-mean scores and orange indicates above-cohort-mean scores for that gene set. The heatmap displays unadjusted individual scores and is descriptive; it is not an independent validation of the selected associations. GSVA, gene set variation analysis; FDR, false discovery rate; CI, confidence interval; GC, glucocorticoid; SONFH, steroid-induced osteonecrosis of the femoral head.
