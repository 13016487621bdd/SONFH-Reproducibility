# Third-party resources

The frozen panel was curated from MSigDB Human release 2026.1.Hs, Hallmark (H) and GO Biological Process (C5:GO:BP) collections. The unchanged selected gene-set membership is preserved for reproducibility; grouping, pairing and analysis labels are study-specific curation. Cite the MSigDB Hallmark paper, GSVA and Gene Ontology references supplied with the manuscript, and the source listed for each gene set.

The panel contains Hallmark and GOBP sets, not the separately restricted KEGG/BioCarta collections. The archive is not a new MSigDB release and the frozen 2026.1.Hs version must not silently be replaced by a newer release.

GEO datasets, platform annotations, and archived source scripts retain their original provenance and terms. This notice grants no new license over those third-party materials. R/Bioconductor/Python packages are dependencies and their source distributions are not repackaged here.
